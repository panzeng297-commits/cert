# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Chinese guests who arrive from social links or direct sharing and need to quickly decide between two Chongqing homestay rooms, understand the price, check basic house rules, and submit a booking request. Most users will browse on mobile and use Chinese map apps for navigation.

## Product Purpose

The site presents two rooms clearly, removes uncertainty before a booking request, and supports a manual confirmation workflow because the host must check Tujia and other platforms before accepting payment.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clean, and locally practical. The page should feel like a refined independent homestay listing, not a content-heavy travel guide or a generic platform listing.

## Anti-references

Avoid repeated generic card sections, separated policy blocks that duplicate room information, vague transportation copy, and a booking flow that forces users to scroll back after choosing a room.

## Design Principles

- Room choice comes before form completion.
- Policies belong where the guest decides to book.
- Facility copy should be concrete and room-specific.
- Navigation should match Chinese mobile habits, including map app choices.
- Visual polish should come from strong imagery, hierarchy, and spacing rather than decorative filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable Chinese text, high contrast, visible focus states, responsive layouts, and native controls where possible. Map and contact actions should remain usable even when a specific map app is not installed.
